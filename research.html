Research

My work sits at the intersection of plant biology, microbial ecology, quantitative analysis, and resilient agriculture.

Root system architecture

I am interested in how root traits vary across genotypes and environments, and how these traits can be quantified to support crop improvement and stress resilience.

Rhizosphere microbiomes

My research also explores microbial communities associated with plant roots, including questions about composition, function, and plant–microbe interactions in crop systems.

Orphan and resilient crops

I am particularly interested in crops that matter for food security and climate adaptation, especially species that remain scientifically under-studied.

Methods and analysis

My work combines experimental biology with image analysis, sequencing workflows, reproducible code, and statistical interpretation.
